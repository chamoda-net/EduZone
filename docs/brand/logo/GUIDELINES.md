# EduZone logo: usage guide

> The Scholar Shield. Chosen 2026-11-24. Colours and fonts in general: `docs/BRANDING.md`.
> See it all in one picture: `presentation/brand-board.png`.

## 1. The logo

**Idea:** a laurel sprig grows straight out of an open book, on a gold-lined academic shield.

| Version | File starts with | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (main) | `eduzone-horizontal` | Site header, receipts, letters, email |
| Stacked | `eduzone-stacked` | Posters, business cards, certificates, square spaces |
| Symbol | `eduzone-symbol` | Where the name is already nearby, 48 px and larger |
| Small cut | `eduzone-symbol-small` | The symbol at 16 to 40 px |
| Wordmark | `eduzone-wordmark` | Where the shield already appears |
| App icon, favicon | `eduzone-app-icon`, `web-icons/` | Phones and browser tabs |

In the logo the name is drawn **EDUZONE**. In writing it is always **EduZone**.

## 2. Which colour version

| Version | Use on |
|---|---|
| `-color` | White, ivory and other light, calm backgrounds |
| `-reversed` | Dark green. It has a gold edge so the shield stays visible |
| `-green` | Light backgrounds, one-colour printing |
| `-black` | Stamps, fax, newspaper, gold backgrounds |
| `-white` | Photos, dark colours that aren't green |

The one-colour versions are real cut-outs, so the background shows through the leaves and the book. They suit
vinyl, engraving and embroidery.

## 3. Space and size

- **Clear space:** on every side, at least the height of the letter **E** in the wordmark. For the symbol alone,
  a quarter of its width.
- **Smallest sizes:**

| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 96 px wide | 24 mm wide |
| Symbol | 48 px high (below that, the small cut) | 10 mm high |
| Small cut | 16 px high (24 px or more is better) | 5 mm high |

At 16 px, prefer the favicon tile to the small shield.

## 4. Colours

| Part | Hex | RGB | CMYK (starting point) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shield | `#124A33` | 18, 74, 51 | 76, 0, 31, 71 |
| Wordmark, icon tile | `#0E3A28` | 14, 58, 40 | 76, 0, 31, 77 |
| Leaves, keyline | `#D4A03F` | 212, 160, 63 | 0, 25, 70, 17 |
| Lit side of the leaves | `#ECD08F` | 236, 208, 143 | 0, 12, 39, 7 |
| Book, wordmark on dark | `#FBFAF5` | 251, 250, 245 | 0, 0, 2, 2 |

CMYK is converted by formula. Ask the printer for a proof. Gold foil may replace the gold on certificates.

## 5. Lettering

The wordmark is Fraunces (weight 560, spaced capitals) converted to outlines, so no font is needed. Fraunces uses
the SIL Open Font License, which allows logo use. Don't retype the name to make the logo.

## 6. Don't

- Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or gradients.
- Change the colours, or make the shield gold or any other colour.
- Put the colour version on photos or busy backgrounds.
- Use the full symbol below 48 px.
- Change the spacing or sizes inside a layout.
- Combine it with a teacher's or institute's own logo.

## 8. Open items

- **Trademark:** not searched. Have a Sri Lankan trademark search and a reverse image search done before launch.
- **Print:** the PDFs are RGB. A printer should convert them to CMYK or spot colours.
- **Cutting and embroidery:** let the supplier test the keyline and the gaps between leaves at the final size.
