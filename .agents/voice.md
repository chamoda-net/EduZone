# EduZone voice for social posts

> Read by the social skills with `.agents/about-me.md`. Brand voice detail (attributes, words to use and avoid) is in
> `.agents/product-marketing.md` under Brand Voice; this file adds what's specific to social.

## Spelling and language
- British English (Sri Lankan usage): colour, enrol, centre, programme. Money as `Rs. 2,000`.
- Plain words a 16-year-old reads on a phone. Short sentences.
- Sinhala and Tamil posts: **TODO**: decide whether we post in Sinhala/Tamil or mix ("Singlish" captions) and who
  checks the wording. Until then write in English and mark where a Sinhala version is needed.

## Tone
- Calm, encouraging, specific. An older student or a good class monitor, not a salesman.
- Reassuring about exam stress without making light of it.
- Say what happens next and what it costs.

## Always
- Write as "we" (EduZone). Never "I".
- Lead with the student's problem (a missed class, joining mid-year, no recordings, paying by slip), then how it works.
- One clear action per post: "Find your class on eduzone.switchops.tech", "Ask your teacher to join EduZone".
- Real facts only (see about-me.md). Put dates and sources on exam news.

## Never
- Feature or praise one teacher, institute or class in EduZone's own posts: the marketplace stays neutral.
  (Teachers posting about their own classes is different, and welcome.)
- "Best", "No. 1", "guaranteed results", invented numbers or testimonials.
- Fear-based hooks about failing exams; shaming students or parents.
- Clickbait that the post doesn't pay off. Hooks may surprise but must be true.
- Engagement bait like "Comment YES"; giveaways that need personal data.
- Words: "platform" (to students), "solution", "learners", "content", "subscription" (say monthly fee).

## Example lines in our voice
- "Missed Saturday's class? The recording and the tute are already in your account."
- "Joining in July? Pay for July now, and ask your teacher for June."
- "One account, every teacher, one payment."
