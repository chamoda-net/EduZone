## Who we are
EduZone (eduzone.switchops.tech) is Sri Lanka's marketplace for online tuition classes: live Zoom lessons,
recordings and tutes from many teachers for Grade 5, O/L and A/L, with one student account and one payment.
Also self-paced video courses with lifetime access. Owned by SwitchOps Tech (Pvt) Ltd. Pre-launch: the pilot with
real tuition teachers is next.

## Who we post for
- **Students** (O/L, A/L, Grade 5 scholarship), mostly on phones: on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook.
- **Parents** who pay, especially for Grade 5 and O/L: on Facebook and WhatsApp.
- **Teachers and institutes** we want to sign up: on Facebook and WhatsApp groups.

## Platforms (not LinkedIn)
Facebook page, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp channel/status. LinkedIn only for company news to
partners and investors. When a skill's format is written for LinkedIn, adapt it to these: shorter text, the hook in
the first line or first 2 seconds, captions on every video, vertical 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 4:5 for feed posts

## Content pillars (proposed; confirm)
1. **Exam-year study help**: revision tips, past-paper habits, how to use recordings, time plans before O/L and A/L.
2. **How EduZone works**: paying month by month, catching up on a missed month, one account for every teacher,
   receipts for parents.
3. **For teachers**: getting paid on time, protecting recordings, a shareable page, the money view.
4. **Exam calendar and news**: exam dates, results days, syllabus changes (facts only, with the source).

## Facts we may use
- Grade 5, O/L and A/L classes; Sinhala, English and Tamil medium (as teachers offer them).
- Pay month by month by card, a receipt per month; several teachers in one cart and one payment.
- Paid months include live Zoom classes, recordings and tutes; joining late: ask the teacher for an earlier month.
- Recordings are protected: watermark with the student's name, one device per account.
- Courses: bought once, lifetime access, refund within 7 days (under 30% watched).
